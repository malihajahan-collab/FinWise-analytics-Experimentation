# FinWise, A Product-Led Growth Strategy

> Get trial users to one personalized cash-flow action quickly, then monetize the ongoing value of keeping that plan current.

## Final Project Deliverables

### Slide 5 · The Bet (Growth Hypothesis)
- **Hypothesis:** If FinWise shows a paid-plan prompt after a user reviews a personalized cash-flow action, trial-to-paid conversion will rise from 1.99% to 2.4%.
- **The bet:** A Revenue-stage PLG bet: place the paid boundary after the Aha moment, not before it.
- **Growth loop:** https://github.com/malihajahan-collab/FinWise-analytics-Experimentation/blob/main/01-bet/growth-bet.md

### Slide 6 · The Solution (Onboarding)
- **Aha moment:** After connecting one account, the user sees a 30-day cash-flow risk and reviews one recommended action.
- **Prototype:** https://finwise-onboarding-p-7fon.bolt.host

### Slide 7 · The Mechanic (Gamification)
- **Mechanic + rationale:** Cash Plan Freshness marks a plan “Needs review” only after a material forecast change. Reviewing the updated action restores it to “Current,” creating a meaningful weekly reason to return.
- **Wireframe:** <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/malihajahan-collab/FinWise-analytics-Experimentation/main/03-mechanic/cash-plan-freshness.svg" alt="Cash Plan Freshness wireframe" />

### Slide 8 · The Signals (Metrics)
- **Leading:** Primary-account import within 24 hours  
Personalized cash-flow action review within seven days of import
- **Lagging:** Trial-to-paid conversion over the full reverse-trial period.
- **Pattern:** Trial-to-paid conversion is flat at 1.87%–2.08%; modeling usage is volatile and weakly correlated with conversion (r = 0.26).

### Slide 9 · The Validation (Experiment)
- **Method:** A standard 50/50 user-level A/B test after the user reviews a personalized cash-flow action.
- **Hypothesis + metric:** Trial-to-paid conversion among eligible users; success is a lift from 1.99% to at least 2.4%.
- **Guardrail + read date:** Seven-day cash-plan review rate cannot fall more than 5% versus control. Read results after the full reverse-trial period; do not stop early.

### Slide 10 · The Model (Pricing)
- **Stage + model:** Stage 2: Revenue Expansion. Use a reverse-trial subscription because FinWise delivers ongoing value as cash forecasts refresh.
- **Recommendation:** Package paid value as Cash Plan Continuity: automatic refreshes, material cash alerts, weekly action reviews, and additional accounts. The first forecast and action remain available in trial.
- **Signal:** The package follows the personalized-action-review leading indicator; trial-to-paid conversion confirms the outcome.

### Slide 11 · The Story (Insights)
- **Friction:** Aggregate data could not show whether the same users reached value and converted, so I defined and instrumented a user-level Aha event.
- **Aha moment:** Account connection and forecasting are setup; value lands when users see a cash-flow risk and know the next action.
- **Takeaways:** Experimentation connects activation, engagement, measurement, validation, and monetization around one value moment.
