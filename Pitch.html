Strategy · M5

The growth bet

Hypothesis

The core belief

If FinWise shows a paid-plan prompt after a user reviews a personalized cash-flow action, trial-to-paid conversion will rise from 1.99% to 2.4%.
The bet

Where we focus

A Revenue-stage PLG bet: place the paid boundary after the Aha moment, not before it.
Activation · M6

The Aha moment

Aha moment

When value lands

After connecting one account, the user sees a 30-day cash-flow risk and reviews one recommended action.
Retention · M7

The habit mechanic

Mechanic

The habit loop

Cash Plan Freshness marks a plan “Needs review” only after a material forecast change. Reviewing the updated action restores it to “Current,” creating a meaningful weekly reason to return.
Metrics · M8

What we measure

Leading

Predicts outcomes

Primary-account import within 24 hours
Personalized cash-flow action review within seven days of import
Lagging

The outcome

Trial-to-paid conversion over the full reverse-trial period.
Data pattern

What it revealed

Trial-to-paid conversion is flat at 1.87%–2.08%; modeling usage is volatile and weakly correlated with conversion (r = 0.26).
Experiment · M9

How we test it

Method

Test design

A standard 50/50 user-level A/B test after the user reviews a personalized cash-flow action.
Hypothesis

Metric + success

Trial-to-paid conversion among eligible users; success is a lift from 1.99% to at least 2.4%.
Guardrails

Limits + read date

Seven-day cash-plan review rate cannot fall more than 5% versus control. Read results after the full reverse-trial period; do not stop early.
Monetization · M10

The model

Stage 2: Revenue Expansion. Use a reverse-trial subscription because FinWise delivers ongoing value as cash forecasts refresh.
Recommendation

The move

Package paid value as Cash Plan Continuity: automatic refreshes, material cash alerts, weekly action reviews, and additional accounts. The first forecast and action remain available in trial.
Model signal

What success looks like

The package follows the personalized-action-review leading indicator; trial-to-paid conversion confirms the outcome.
Reflection · M11

What I learned

Friction

The hard part

Aggregate data could not show whether the same users reached value and converted, so I defined and instrumented a user-level Aha event.
Aha

The moment it clicked

Account connection and forecasting are setup; value lands when users see a cash-flow risk and know the next action.
Takeaways

What stuck

Experimentation connects activation, engagement, measurement, validation, and monetization around one value moment.
